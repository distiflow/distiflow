Spend effort where it changes the outcome.
- Use the cheapest model that can do a role; keep the strongest for planning and review.
- Read files in windows: search first, then read the part you need.
- Hand large artifacts over as files, not pasted text.
- End a session at a natural boundary rather than pushing a long one further.
Why: cost grows faster than length, and quality falls with it.
